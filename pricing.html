Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use the free plan?

No — anyone can generate and download a QR code or barcode instantly with no sign-up. Creating a free account additionally unlocks colorful presets and gradients.

What does the watermark look like?

Free and free-account QR codes include a small "EzyQRGen.com" credit strip under the code. It doesn't affect scannability. Pro removes it entirely.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription you can cancel whenever you like from your Stripe billing portal — no long-term commitment.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major debit and credit cards, processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
